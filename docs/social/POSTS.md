### C3: Myth vs Fact: trades edition
- **Format:** carousel, 6 slides (cover, 4 myths, sources/CTA)
- **Platforms:** Instagram, Threads, LinkedIn
- **Caption:**
  > 4 things people get wrong about trades, checked against the data.
  >
  > 1. "Trades are for people who couldn't get the ATAR." 94.8% of trade apprentices who finish are employed after training.
  > 2. "Everyone quits." 59.4% of construction trade apprentices who started in 2019 finished within six years (electrotech: 68.3%).
  > 3. "Sites are mostly workers on visas." 24% of construction workers were born overseas, vs 32% of all workers. Only 0.5% are on temporary skilled visas.
  > 4. "There's no work." 21,600 unfilled construction jobs in Feb 2026.
  >
  > Uni or trade, both are legit. The free calculator shows the trade-path numbers. Link in bio.
- **Hashtags:** #mythvsfact #tradies #apprenticeship #careersadvice #construction
- **Alt text:** Myth vs fact carousel. Each slide shows a myth in grey and a highlighted fact number: 94.8% employed after finishing, 59.4% six-year completion, 24% overseas-born vs 32% overall, 21,600 unfilled construction jobs. The last slide lists sources.
- **Sources:** NCVER Apprentice and Trainee Outcomes 2025; NCVER completion rates 2025 (2019 cohort); Grattan Institute (2021 Census); ABS Job Vacancies Feb 2026; HIA Trades Availability June qtr 2026.
