# Apprentice Guide: social voice guide (v2, 17 Nov 2026)

This is modelled on what makes high-performing Australian data carousels work (for example, @theaussiecorporate's "10 things nobody tells you about your pay": about 6,000 likes and 5,700 shares). We copy the *writing pattern*, never their branding, mascot or charts.

## The pattern, slide by slide
1. **Cover:** a curiosity list title in sentence case, with no number in the hook except the list count.
   "10 things nobody tells you about apprentice pay". A small caps kicker sits above it ("APPRENTICE GUIDE · 2026–27 PAY GUIDE"), and a "READ MORE ▸" pill sits at the bottom.
2. **Insight slides (one idea each):**
   - **Tag pill:** "ONE", "TWO"… (the number as a word, nothing else).
   - **Headline:** ONE blunt declarative sentence, at most 10 words. Second person or a plain fact, present tense. A two-beat contrast is welcome ("Chippies out-earn sparkies for four years. Then sparkies overtake."). It states the finding, not the topic.
   - **Subhead:** ONE sentence of evidence with the specific numbers. Plain words.
   - **Chart:** one per slide, with a caps label above it ("WEEKLY PAY BY YEAR OF APPRENTICESHIP"). Values sit on the bars or points in short form ($654, $1.1K, 68%). Use orange for the hero series and blueprint blue or steel for comparisons.
   - **Kicker line at the bottom:** a **bold first clause** with the "so what", then a plain second clause with a comparison or consequence.
   - **"SWIPE TO SEE NEXT ▸"** pill bottom-right.
3. **Final slide: the soft sell.** "If only there was a tool…", then a calculator mockup, then "…that told you what you'd actually take home.", then "It's free. Link in bio." Sources go here, small, in one block.

## Rules
- **Confident, not hedged.** Slides must not contain "award minimum", "illustrative", "junior, 4-yr, no Year 12", "may", "usually", "eligible occupations", or brackets of caveats. Put the conditions in the subhead's own wording ("A 17-year-old first-year chippy earns…") and put the fine print in the caption and on the final slide.
- **Specific numbers, rounded for reading:** $654 a week, $5,800, 69%, 1 in 3, 4 in 10, 8 in 10. Prefer "X in 10" and "1 in X" framings. Weekly dollars for pay, yearly for totals.
- **Every number is true.** It comes from `site/js/rates.js` or a verified source and is computed correctly. Rounding is fine; spin isn't.
- **Sources stay off the insight slides.** They go on the final slide and in the caption.
- **Words:** everyday, Australian ("chippy", "sparky", "tradie" are fine). No moralising ("both are legit"), no jokes that need explaining, no exclamation marks.
- **Sentence case** for headlines. Anton is for big numbers and the cover only. Headlines use Inter 800 in sentence case, which reads like a confident editorial voice.
- **Alternate backgrounds** slide to slide (concrete, then ink, then concrete…) so the swipe feels like motion.

## Caption pattern
Line 1 repeats the hook with a twist: "10 things nobody tells you about apprentice pay in Australia…". Then three or four short lines teasing the best insights. Then "Free take-home pay calculator: link in bio." Then a block of fine-print conditions and sources. 3–5 hashtags.

## Tweet and stat cards in this voice
Same rules. A tweet card is one claim plus one number plus one consequence, three short paragraphs at most. No caveats in the card itself.
