# Apprentice Guide: "what's happening with trades" explainer Reels (research batch 3, 18 Nov 2026)

These are drafts only; nothing has been posted. They're modelled on Joseph's @moneydecoded.au reference (30s, 30.3K likes, 18.5K shares). We copy the **structure**, not their art or branding:
- one physical metaphor per beat on a dark set;
- a calm voiceover with small captions at the top;
- a glowing "So, what happened?" pivot;
- a cause → effect → who-gets-hurt chain;
- no face, no CTA, no site or calculator plug.

**Reference breakdown (from `ref3/s1.jpg` and `full.jpg`).** Each beat has one hero object, centred, lit from above on near-black. The number sits *on* the object: "5%" on the cart, "20%" on the gauge, the counter under the tray. Colour carries meaning: the cart turns red at "the first to get hurt". The pivot card is the only full-screen text. The final beat is a counter rolling up, which is the shareable stat.

**Rules applied.**
- Every on-screen number comes from a primary source (or HIA/Master Builders) opened on 17–18 Nov 2026. The source and date are listed per beat.
- Anything I couldn't verify against a primary page is listed under "Kept out" and appears in no storyboard.
- Spoken numbers are written as words.
- Timings assume about 2.4 spoken words per second.

**Why this order:**
- #1 and #2 hit the two things every adult worries about, housing and cost, using primary NHSAC/ABS numbers.
- #3 and #4 are the most "trades" specific.
- #10 drops because the consequence for home buyers, the most shareable part, is the one thing I couldn't source. I didn't verify any figure for homes left unfinished.

**Topic starters dropped or flipped:**
- **"An ageing trade workforce":** JSA data contradicts it. Trades are *younger* than the workforce overall (carpenters 30, electricians 32, plumbers 34, against 39 for all jobs). It's reframed as concept 7.
- **"Migration and skilled visas":** no primary share or visa figure verified this pass, so no concept was built.
- **"Construction cost index":** folded into #2, using NHSAC's house construction cost measure and ABS CPI.
