# Apprentice Guide: social content plan (v1, 17 Nov 2026)

**Goal:** get parents, careers advisers, career changers and small trade business owners to use the calculators, join the newsletter and send enquiries.

**Approach:** posts without a face on camera, so you can make them without being a tradie: carousels, screen recordings of the calculator, voiceover over stock or b-roll, text on screen. Add real apprentices and tradies later as collaborators. Only feature people aged 18+, and get written permission.

**Platforms:**
- Instagram and Threads (carousels and Reels)
- TikTok and YouTube Shorts (the same vertical videos)
- LinkedIn (data posts aimed at employers and careers advisers)
- Facebook trade groups (calculators as genuinely useful tools; follow each group's rules)

**Rules:**
- Every number comes from the site's rates file and carries its date ("2026–27 award minimum").
- Say "minimum", not "what you'll earn".
- No promises about visas or jobs.
- Australian slang is fine. Don't punch down at uni students or tradies; the tone is "both are legit, here are the real numbers".

## Content pillars (mix about 60% education, 40% fun)

### 1. Payslip Reveal (education, the main series)
Show the real take-home pay for a trade, apprentice year and age, straight from the calculator.
- "What a 1st-year carpentry apprentice actually takes home in NSW (2026–27)." Screen-record the calculator and reveal the weekly figure last.
- "Year 1 → Year 4 sparky: watch the pay climb." An animated bar per year.
- "Adult apprentice vs junior apprentice: why starting at 25 pays differently."
- "Where does the $10,000 go? The Key Apprenticeship Program payment timeline": 6 months, 12, 24, 36, completion.

### 2. Trade vs Uni at 25 (education, the most shareable)
Show the calculator chart: two lines, the trade path and the uni path, and where they cross.
- "Same 17-year-old, two paths. Who's ahead at 25?" Edit the assumptions live on screen.
- "HECS vs tools: what each path costs before you earn."
- "The uni path wins if… / the trade path wins if…" Show it fairly, as a balanced carousel.
- Poll: "Guess the gap at 25." Reveal it the next day.

### 3. Myth vs Fact (education)
Quick carousels, each backed by a source.
- "Myth: trades are for people who couldn't get the ATAR." Fact: 94.8% of trade apprentices who finish are employed after training (NCVER 2025).
- "Myth: everyone quits apprenticeships." Fact: about 6 in 10 construction apprentices finish within six years (NCVER, 2019 cohort: 59.4%).
- "Myth: Australia is full of migrant builders." Fact: construction has a *lower* share of overseas-born workers than the workforce overall (Grattan, 2021 Census).
- "Myth: there's no work." Fact: 21,600 unfilled construction jobs (Feb 2026) and ongoing trade shortages (HIA).

### 4. Apprentice Survival Guide (education aimed at apprentices finishing; feeds the employer tool later)
- "5 things I wish I knew in my first 90 days on site."
- "Your apprentice rights in 60 seconds": pay, super, training time, who to call (Fair Work, your Apprenticeship Network Provider).
- "Tool budget for a 1st-year: what you actually need vs what Instagram tells you."
- "Thinking of quitting? Read this first": switching employer, Group Training Organisations, mentoring support.

### 5. Employer Corner (B2B, LinkedIn and Facebook groups)
- "What does an apprentice *really* cost a small builder in 2026–27?" Calculator walkthrough.
- "Incentives most small businesses never claim." Checklist carousel.
- "Why apprentices quit (it's mostly not the money)." Use the NCVER reasons with a practical fix for each.
- "Group Training Organisation vs hiring direct: which suits a 2-person business?"
- End every post with: "Join the Employer Dashboard waitlist." This is the fake-door test for the paid tool.

### 6. Career Changer (education, adults 25–40)
- "Got laid off from corporate. Should I become a sparky at 30?" Honest maths using the adult apprentice rate.
- "From spreadsheets to spirit levels": a series of interviews with career changers (collaborations).
- "The 3 questions to ask before you quit your desk job for a trade."

### 7. Fun / entertainment (reach and shares)
- **"Guess the trade"**: a close-up of a tool or a weird site photo; viewers guess the trade. Answer in the comments.
- **"Tradie translator"**: site slang explained (smoko, arvo, hoo-roo, "she'll be right" vs what it actually means). Light-hearted, nothing offensive.
- **"Rate my smoko"**: viewers send in smoko spreads and we rate them. Low effort, high engagement.
- **"Uni lecture vs site briefing"**: a split-screen skit with the same information delivered two ways.
- **"POV: your first day as an apprentice"**: a relatable trope video, e.g. "go get the left-handed hammer". Make it kind, not mean.
- **"How it's made, Aussie house edition"**: time-lapse or stock of slab → frame → roof → handover, with "who did each bit" labels. Educational and satisfying.
- **"Tradie pay bingo"**: guess the hourly minimum for 6 trades, then reveal.
- **"Things your careers adviser never told you"**: punchy and slightly cheeky, but still sourced.

### 8. Data drops (weekly "By the numbers" format, Boring Businesses style)
A text-only carousel with 1 claim per slide and the source on the final slide:
- "Which trades are the hardest to find right now?" HIA trades availability, quarterly.
- "Women are ~3% of construction tradies and the biggest untapped workforce."
- "Australia builds half as many homes per hour worked as in 1995" (Productivity Commission, 2025).
- "The 1.2 million homes target needs ~116,700 more workers" (BuildSkills, Sept 2025).
- Reply when the government, NCVER or HIA publish new figures. Being fast on new data is the edge.

## What to measure
- Calculator completions per post (put UTM tags on the link in bio)
- Newsletter sign-ups
- Enquiry form submissions
- Employer waitlist sign-ups
- Saves and shares (better than likes for showing what's useful)

Kill a series that gets low saves after 4 posts; double down on the top 2.
