Its figures are compiled estimates, not official data, so the source lines name the guide ("cost guide" for Q1–Q2, "(national)" for Q3).
- **Q4:** this is one company's published formula. The screen says "A Sydney reliner's published prices" and doesn't name the company. Other Sydney relining companies use similar setup + per-metre pricing.
- **Q5:** a real quote from a public review (a Victorian customer), used as the twist. The business is not named anywhere on screen or in the caption. The review page shows only a relative date, so the screen says "2025" with no month or weekday. Before posting, screenshot the review and keep it in the project folder as a record. The "fair" range is an estimate built from two cost guides, and the screen says "about". The fair range comes from Sydney and NSW guides, while the quote is Victorian; prices are broadly similar, but treat it as a ballpark.
