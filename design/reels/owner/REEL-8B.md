## Structure

1. **Hook (0–6.5s):** Accountant $2,003 a week vs plumber $1,990 a week. "ABOUT THE SAME PAY. No degree needed."
2. **Problem (6.5–15s):** Two paths from school: uni → corporate → $$ ("What we're told"), and trade → own it → $$$ ("The other path").
3. **Example (15–32.5s):** Training (3-year degree vs 4-year apprenticeship); while training (−$52,197 HELP debt at 2026 rates vs +$173,672 paid to learn, award minimum, pre-tax); typical pay, median full-time across all ages ($104K vs $103K a year); median full-time job $96K, "Both out-earn most workers."
4. **Twist (32.5–49s):** The plumber starts a business. One plumber: $125/hr × 1,380 billable hours = $172,500 revenue a year. Team of five: $862,500. Labelled "Example · before wages, materials and costs" from the moment the numbers appear.
5. **Opportunity (49–63.5s):** "Now's the time." 1.2M homes to build; trade apprentice starts down three years in a row; 20K construction jobs open right now. Demand ▲ Supply ▼.
6. **Call to action (63.5–71s):** "How does your pay stack up?" Bars for plumber, accountant, electrician and "YOU?". Comment TRADES for the free pay calculator.

## Caption

A plumber earns about the same as an accountant. Then the plumber starts a business. 👷‍♂️💼

📚 Accountant: 3-year degree, about $52K in HELP debt at 2026 rates (if fees are deferred), and a median full-time pay of about $104K a year.
🔧 Plumber: paid about $174K before tax while training (award minimum, 4 years), and a median full-time pay of about $103K a year.

Those medians cover all ages. Pay straight out of uni or an apprenticeship starts lower: business graduates' median is $74K, and a newly qualified plumber's award minimum is about $65K.

🚀 The twist: a plumber can run their own business. One plumber billing $125/hr for 1,380 hours is $172,500 a year in revenue. A team of five is $862,500. (Example only. Revenue isn't profit: wages, materials and running costs come out first.)

📈 And demand is strong: Australia is aiming for 1.2 million new homes, trade apprentice starts have fallen three years in a row, and about 20,000 construction jobs are open right now.

💬 Comment TRADES and we'll DM you our free calculator to see how your pay stacks up.

General information only, not financial advice. Uni is still a great path for many people; this is about knowing there's more than one.

Sources: Jobs and Skills Australia occupation profiles (ABS Employee Earnings and Hours, May 2025): accountants $2,003, plumbers $1,990, electricians $2,191, all jobs $1,852 median full-time a week.
