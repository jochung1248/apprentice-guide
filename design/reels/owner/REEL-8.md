## Comment-to-DM setup

ManyChat's free plan caps you at 25 contacts a month, which one decent Reel can blow through in hours. Two options:

- **ManyChat Pro** (from about US$15–17/month, priced by contacts). The most used, most tutorials.
- **A free alternative** with no contact cap (Elev8or AutoDM, Beacons Smart Replies, CreatorFlow 500 DMs/month). Check current limits before choosing.

Steps (same idea in any tool):
1. Make sure @apprenticeguide is a Business or Creator account, and in Instagram settings allow access to messages for connected tools.
2. Host the PDF somewhere with a stable link (e.g. `apprenticeguide.com.au/boss.pdf` on the site, or a Google Drive link set to "Anyone with the link").
3. New automation → trigger **"User comments on your post or Reel"** → pick this Reel → keyword **BOSS** (contains, any case). Add a second trigger for a DM containing BOSS.
4. Turn on a public reply to the comment, e.g. "Sent! Check your DMs 📘" (set 2–3 variations so it doesn't look spammy).
5. DM message: "G'day! Here's your free Tradie Boss Starter guide 👷 [button: Get the guide → link]". Optional: ask them to follow first, or ask for an email to build a list.
6. Test from a second account before posting.
