Not included: overtime, penalty rates, travel and other allowances outside the pay guide rate, leave loading, payroll tax (only applies above your state's threshold), the supervision time of a qualified worker, and state employer incentives (listed separately above). An apprentice also does productive work that brings money in, but we don't try to estimate that. Check the apprentice's exact entitlements with the Fair Work Pay Calculator.

Not ready to employ directly? Consider a GTO

A Group Training Organisation (GTO) is the legal employer of the apprentice and "hosts" them out to your business. You pay the GTO an hourly charge that covers wages, super, workers comp, leave and admin, and the GTO handles recruitment, paperwork and pastoral care. If work dries up, the GTO can move the apprentice to another host so their training continues. It can cost more per hour than employing directly, but it takes most of the risk and admin off a small business — useful for a first apprentice. Ask any GTO for a quote and compare it with the figure above.
