Why we built this

95%

of trade apprentices who finished were working after their training, according to the NCVER 2025 outcomes survey as cited by the federal Skills Minister.

$10,000

is the most a full-time housing-construction apprentice can receive through the Key Apprenticeship Program, paid in five instalments.

4 in 10

trade apprentices who started in 2021 had dropped out within four years, as reported from NCVER data. Pay, conditions and the workplace are common reasons.†
