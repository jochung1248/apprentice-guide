Key Apprenticeship Program payments

    Extra help in your state

    These are minimums. Many employers pay above award. Overtime, penalty rates, travel, fares and most other allowances aren't included (the pay guide rates already include the industry and tool allowances where they apply). Tax is an end-of-year estimate for an Australian resident with no other income or deductions, so your pay slip may differ slightly. Check your exact rate with the Fair Work Pay Calculator.

    Good to know

    • Rates shown are for apprentices who started on or after 1 January 2014, on weekly hire. Building trades use the residential rates; non-residential (commercial) rates are slightly higher.
    • Under-18s pay normal resident tax rates on wages — the higher minors' rates only apply to some other income, such as certain trust distributions.
    • Pay rates are national award minimums, so they're the same in every state and in the city or the regions. In WA, some small employers (sole traders and partnerships) use WA state awards instead.
    • Moving out of home for an apprenticeship? You might be eligible for a Living Away From Home Allowance — see the incentives guide.
