Short answer: on the 2026–27 award minimums, a full-time first-year junior apprentice in a building, electrical or plumbing trade earns between $622.72 and $710.45 a week before tax, and keeps roughly $587 to $656 after tax. Adult apprentices (21 and over when they start) earn a lot more. Here's the detail, trade by trade.

Why the "NSW" rate is a national rate

Most apprentices in NSW building trades are covered by national modern awards, not a state award. So a first-year carpentry apprentice in Penrith has the same minimum as one in Perth. The three awards that matter here are the Building and Construction General On-site Award (carpenters, joiners, bricklayers, tilers and more), the Electrical, Electronic and Communications Contracting Award (electricians) and the Plumbing and Fire Sprinklers Award (plumbers).

The Fair Work Commission raised award rates by 4.75% from the first full pay period on or after 1 July 2026. All the figures below come from the Fair Work Ombudsman pay guides published after that increase.

A worked example: first-year carpentry apprentice

Mia is 17, left school after Year 11 and starts a carpentry apprenticeship with a builder in western Sydney.

  1. Weekly award minimum: $654.49 for 38 hours.
  2. Yearly pay: $654.49 × 52 = $34,033.48. Apprentices get paid annual leave and public holidays, so we count all 52 weeks.
  3. Income tax: the first $18,200 is tax-free and the next band is taxed at 15% (down from 16% last year). That's $2,374.95, but the low income tax offset knocks off $700, leaving $1,674.95.
  4. Medicare levy: at this income the levy is still being phased in, so it's $602.20 rather than the full 2%.
  5. Take-home: $34,033.48 − $1,674.95 − $602.20 = $31,756.33 a year, or about $610.70 a week ($1,221.40 a fortnight).

Her pay slip may show a slightly different amount of tax each week, because employers use the ATO's withholding tables; any difference is sorted out at tax time.

Three things that change the number

1. Finishing Year 12

The awards pay junior apprentices who finished Year 12 more in their first two years. For a first-year electrical apprentice the difference is $60.04 a week before tax ($682.76 versus $622.72). From third year the rates are the same.

2. Being an adult apprentice

If you're 21 or older when you start, you're an adult apprentice and the minimum jumps — for a carpenter, to $1,108.44 a week in every year of the apprenticeship. That's why trades are a realistic career change in your twenties and thirties (see our career-changer's guide).

3. Your employer

These are minimums. Plenty of builders and contractors pay first-years above award, especially in a tight labour market, and overtime, travel allowances and site allowances can add more. If you're offered a job, it's fair to ask what the hourly rate is and how it rises each year.

It gets better quickly

Apprentice minimums step up every year. A junior carpentry apprentice goes from $654.49 a week in first year to $1,102.13 in fourth year, and a qualified carpenter's award minimum is $1,214.04 a week. Electricians move from $622.72 to $1,006.97 a week by fourth year, then to $1,223.11 once licensed.

On top of wages, most housing-construction apprentices who started from 1 July 2025 can get up to $10,000 through the Key Apprenticeship Program, paid in five $2,000 instalments. Our KAP guide explains who qualifies.

Frequently asked questions

Do under-18 apprentices pay less tax?
No. Wages earned by under-18s are taxed at normal resident rates, including the $18,200 tax-free threshold. The higher "minor" tax rates only apply to things like interest and dividends.
Are NSW apprentice rates different from other states?
For apprentices covered by these national awards, no — the minimums are the same across Australia. Some states offer extra support, such as fee-free training, on top.
Does the award rate include allowances?
The pay guide rates already include the industry and tool allowances where they apply. Travel, fares, overtime and penalty rates are extra.
Where can I check my exact rate?
Use the Fair Work Ombudsman's Pay Calculator, or call them on 13 13 94. It's free and confidential.
