Assumptions

    This is a model, not a prediction. We hold tax rates and HELP thresholds at 2026–27 levels and assume steady full-time work. Real careers vary a lot: overtime, running your own business, a slower first job, or a big graduate pay jump can all change the answer. Money isn't everything either — pick the work you'll enjoy doing.
